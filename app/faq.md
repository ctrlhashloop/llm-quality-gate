# Acme Bank FAQ (fictional)

## Loans
- Maximum personal loan: KES 500,000.
- Repayment period: 6 to 36 months.
- Interest: 14% per year on a reducing balance.
- Late repayment fee: KES 500 per missed instalment.
- Applicants must be 18 or older, with a salary account active for at least 3 months.
- Loan decisions are given within 2 working days.
- Early repayment is allowed with no penalty.

## Accounts and cards
- Minimum balance on a savings account: KES 1,000.
- Card replacement fee: KES 1,000.
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: KES 40,000.
- Lost cards can be blocked 24 hours a day via the USSD menu or by calling customer care.

## Branches
- Branch hours: Monday to Friday 8:30am to 4:30pm; Saturday 9am to 1pm.
- Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
